Scope and transparency

What this statement covers

Accessibility can vary by app and operating-system version. This statement covers Magic Pulse-owned interfaces in the current release.

  • Magic Pulse interfaceCore park, ride, planning, alerts, settings, and Apple Watch views are included.
  • Apple system screensPurchases, permissions, notifications, and sharing can move into interfaces controlled by Apple.
  • Live park informationThird-party sources may change names or wording, while Magic Pulse controls how that information is labeled and presented.

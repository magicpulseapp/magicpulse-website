FAQ

Common questions

Is Magic Pulse affiliated with park operators?

No. Magic Pulse is an independent app and is not endorsed by Disney, Universal, or other park operators.

Where does wait data come from?

Wait times and park hours are sourced from ThemeParks Wiki. The app and website may use MagicPulseAPI to aggregate and display snapshots.

Is the app free?

Yes. Free includes core planning; Pro adds alerts, widgets, Live Activity, and Shortcuts.

Which parks are supported?

Walt Disney World, Disneyland Resort, Tokyo Disney Resort, and Disneyland Paris—multiple parks per region in one app.

Does the website show real waits?

The hero panel loads a live snapshot from the public Magic Pulse API when available. If the default park is closed, we try another open park automatically.

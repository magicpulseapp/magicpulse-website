Overview

Magic Pulse (“we”, “our”) provides live theme park planning information. This policy describes what data is involved in the iOS app, the Apple Watch app, the optional Magic Pulse API, and this website.

Data used in the iOS app

  • Location (optional): Used to suggest nearby parks and to deliver location-based alerts you enable. We do not sell your location.
  • Notifications: Used only for alerts you opt into, including drop alerts, Lightning Lane price alerts, day-plan reminders, park-opening alerts, and severe-weather notifications. You can turn them off individually or globally in Settings.
  • On-device preferences: Watchlist, thresholds, display settings, and similar preferences are stored on your device.
  • iCloud settings sync: If you’re signed in to iCloud, certain preferences sync across your own iPhones and iPads via Apple’s Key-Value Store. The data is stored in your private iCloud account; we never see it. You can disable iCloud Drive for Magic Pulse to opt out.
  • Day plans: Plans are stored locally in Core Data. When you choose to share a plan, that plan is mirrored to your private iCloud database via Apple’s CloudKit so invited family members can collaborate. Magic Pulse never holds a copy of your CloudKit data.
  • On-device history (forecasts): Wait-time history used for trends and on-device forecasts stays on your device unless you use the optional API path below.
  • Optional Magic Pulse API: If you or the default configuration use our servers, the app may send requests authenticated with Apple’s App Attest and an app API key. We process park snapshots and aggregated wait history to power forecasts, Lightning Lane price history, and crowd insights. We do not use this to build an advertising profile.
  • Advertising (free tier): The free tier may show banner ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage data as described in Google’s Privacy Policy. Magic Pulse Pro removes the in-app banner.
  • Diagnostics: Lightweight app events may be logged locally on your device for product improvement and are not uploaded by default.

Apple Watch app

The Apple Watch companion is read-only. It receives a snapshot of your selected park’s name, hours, crowd level, and top watchlist ride wait from the iPhone via Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. No data leaves the watch independently: there’s no network connection, no separate account, and no analytics.

Data we do not sell

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party behavioral advertising beyond what ad networks apply when ads are shown on the free tier.

Third-party services

  • Wait times and park operating data: ThemeParks Wiki and related public park data.
  • Weather: Apple Weather via WeatherKit in the app.
  • Settings sync and shared day plans: Apple iCloud Key-Value Store and CloudKit.
  • Purchases: Apple App Store subscriptions and one-time purchases.
  • Ads: Google AdMob on the free tier.

This website

The marketing site may load a read-only live snapshot from our API or from ThemeParks Wiki; no account is required. Fonts are self-hosted — the site makes no requests to Google Fonts. The support form and Android waitlist are processed by our form provider (Formspree) when you submit them; only the fields you enter and the form’s purpose labels are sent. Android waitlist emails are used only for meaningful Android testing or availability updates.

Children

Magic Pulse is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can limit location and notifications in iOS Settings. For ad-related settings, use Apple’s “Tracking” and in-app privacy options where available. California residents may have additional rights under the CPRA; contact us via the support form and we will respond as required.

International users

If you use the app or site from outside the United States, data may be processed in the U.S. or where our service providers operate.

Retention

Server-side wait samples and snapshots are kept only as long as needed to operate the service and improve reliability, including automatic pruning of old history. Support emails or form submissions are retained only as needed to respond and meet legal obligations. Android waitlist addresses are retained until the list’s purpose ends or you ask us to remove your address.

Policy updates

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the current version. Material changes will be reflected here and, where appropriate, in the app.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the support form. Choose topic: Privacy.

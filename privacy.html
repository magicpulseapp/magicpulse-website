Privacy Policy

Magic Pulse is designed to be privacy-first. No account is required to use the app, and your personal data is never sold.

Last updated: March 21, 2026

Overview

Magic Pulse provides live Disney wait times and theme park planning tools with a minimal data footprint. The sections below describe what data is used and why.

Data used in the app

  • Location (optional): Used only to suggest nearby parks and to enable location-based alerts if you choose to turn them on. Location is never stored on our servers.
  • Notifications: Used only for Smart Alerts you explicitly enable — for example, when a watchlist ride drops below a wait threshold.
  • On-device preferences: Your watchlist, display settings, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard storage APIs. This data is not transmitted to Magic Pulse servers.

Data we do not collect or sell

Magic Pulse does not require an account. We do not collect, store, or sell personal data for advertising or third-party profiling purposes.

Third-party services

  • Wait and park data: Sourced from ThemeParks Wiki, a community-maintained open data project.
  • Weather data: Provided by Apple Weather via the WeatherKit framework. Governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • In-app purchases: Processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. Magic Pulse does not handle or store payment information.

This website

The marketing site may load a live wait snapshot from the Magic Pulse API. That request is read-only and does not require an account or any personal data. Support form submissions are sent via Formspree only when you choose to submit — see Formspree's privacy policy for how they handle form data.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the app or website changes in ways that affect data handling. The date at the top of this page reflects the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, use the support form and select "Privacy" as the topic.
